Status: **pending / bekleniyor**. Do not mark complete from a screenshot or automated browser result alone.

1. Disable YTM AI Blocker and close any older Amber VU windows.
2. Extract the Windows ZIP. Open START-Windows.cmd or START.html.
3. Load the extension folder at chrome://extensions. Confirm version 0.4.3.
4. Pin Amber VU. Open YouTube, play a song, then click Amber VU in that tab.
5. Confirm the author name and email are visible, sound continues without doubling, and L/R meters respond.
6. Try all 15 themes. Verify Nixie time, paper feed/rolls, Tesla sphere and the Vintage power lamp.
7. Test Dinlemeyi durdur, then Yeniden başlat. Close/reopen the visualizer.
8. Play a video for at least five minutes with Radial Stereo and Tesla. Confirm video and sound do not stall. Hide the visualizer and compare playback.
9. Change tracks, pause/resume and close the source tab. Confirm capture ends when expected.

Record: Windows version, browser name/version, extension version, audio output device, source URL, observed behavior and any console errors. Do not include passwords, tokens or personal browser data.
